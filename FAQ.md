**Dress code**: Formal dress is kindly requested for the wedding - long dresses or dressy separates for women and suits for men. There is no dress code for the welcome dinner, but cocktail attire is fine.

**Transportation**: the closest (only major airport is Serbia) is Belgrade Airport Nikola Tesla; there are direct flights from NYC and Chicago (not necessarily every day), as well as daily flights to/from all major European cities. For transportation between Belgrade & airport, as well as Belgrade & Novi Sad, the easiest option is hiring a car service; transfer between airport and Belgrade should be around $30-$40 one way, while transfer between Belgrade/airport and Novi Sad should be around $90 one way. Note that there is no Ubers/Lifts in Serbia, cabs are the most common way of transportation.

**Attractions in Novi Sad**: Novi Sad has a really nice city center (where the welcome party as well as the ceremony are), with few nice parks and churches. Besides that we’d recommend taking a stroll by the Danube river, as well as visiting historic Petrovaradin fortress that’s right across the river.
