Kirk and Laura took a scouting trip to Novi Sad to personally test out different hotels and find the best recommendations for wedding guests. The boutique options below were hand-picked based on their visits — here's what they found.

## A Few Notes About Hotels in Serbia

Hotels in Serbia can be a bit different from what you might expect in the U.S. Reception areas may be more modest, and queen or king beds often come with two separate individual-sized blankets and one pillow per person. None of the boutique hotels below have a traditional lobby — some even require you to check in at their attached restaurant — and check-in hours may be limited, so be sure to coordinate your arrival time with the hotel.

One thing to note about Serbia: smoking is allowed everywhere, including cafes, restaurants, and bars. However, the hotel rooms were not smoky. Since the wedding is in summer, most time will be spent at outdoor cafes, which helps minimize exposure.

We recommend staying in Novi Sad's beautiful city center, which features a large pedestrian area with cafes, small shops, and the church where our ceremony will be held. This area is also walkable to our welcome dinner on Thursday evening. We've chosen small boutique hotels and rooms that capture the history and spirit of the area — while they're not fancy, Kirk and Laura visited each one and can vouch for their charm. The people in Novi Sad are very friendly and almost always speak English.

## Boutique Hotels in the Heart of Downtown

*Traditional Serbian accommodations in the lovely pedestrianized plaza near the church*

**Fontana Hotel** (19 rooms)
A small hotel just steps outside the pedestrian zone on a quiet street in a charming historic building with a restaurant and outdoor terrace. Some rooms have balconies. Simple, clean rooms.

**Veliki Hotel** (14 rooms)
Well-located across from the Fontana and attached to a popular restaurant with the same name. Note that some rooms are updated while others are not.

**Kombinat Rooms** (20 rooms)
Well located at the edge of the pedestrian zone less than 2 blocks from the church with a modern, contemporary feel. All rooms are recently renovated. 10 rooms face a busy avenue and are on the smaller side; the other 10 face a pedestrian plaza but have very high windows — you'll get sunlight but may see only sky (and the church steeple, which adds charm). Check-in is at a front desk and is attended 24 hours. Breakfast is included at the popular cafe next door, which has an inviting outdoor terrace in summer.

Rooms are held for wedding guests — **book directly with the hotel and mention the Brett wedding.** Rates are 95–110 EUR/night including tax and breakfast. Contact Tijana at info@kombinatfood.rs.

**Lanterna Rooms** (8 rooms)
Lovely, clean and bright rooms above a popular pizza restaurant at the edge of the pedestrian zone (about 4 blocks from the church) and near the entrance to the must-see Dunavski Park. Check-in is at the restaurant. The staff is warm and accommodating, and the hotel is highly rated on Booking.com. Note: one room has no windows, so be sure to confirm your room type when booking. Rates are approximately $100–120/night including taxes and breakfast. Book on Booking.com.

**Corso Rooms** (8 rooms)
Kirk and Laura's favorite from the scouting trip! Rooms above a popular cafe in the heart of the pedestrian zone, recently refurbished with comfortable beds and nice showers. There are only 4 rooms with exterior windows — the other 4 have windows opening onto the interior hallway. Be sure to request an exterior-facing room when booking. Exterior rooms are 65 EUR/night including taxes and breakfast. Book directly by emailing office@corso.rs and mention the Brett wedding.

**Arhiv** (9 rooms)
Contemporary, modern rooms just off the pedestrian plaza adjacent to a charming old-world restaurant (where breakfast is served) and a lively courtyard with bustling outdoor cafes. Note: the entrance from the street passes through an archway with graffiti, but the rooms themselves are renovated and pleasant. Check-in hours are limited, so coordinate your arrival time with the owner in advance. Rooms sleep 1–4 people and are priced at 60–110 EUR/night. Book directly at hotelarhiv@gmail.com and mention the Brett wedding.

**Queen Palace**
Renovated rooms in a historic but dated building immediately next door to the church and pedestrian plaza. Lovely rooms with comfortable beds and shared balconies overlooking the city rooftops. Note: the entrance is through an archway leading to a small elevator, which may feel a bit off-putting at first, but the rooms are nice.

## Full-Service Hotels

*American-style hotels, separated from the pedestrianized plaza by a busy road*

**Hotel Pupin**
Large, fully renovated rooms in a full-service hotel with two restaurants, walk-in closets, and high-end amenities. Located in an office building on the main road — you enter through the office building and take an elevator up to reception. To reach the pedestrian zone and church, you'll walk through an underground tunnel. The experience feels more business-oriented than charming, but the rooms are comfortable and modern. Rates are approximately 150 EUR/night.

**Hotel Centar**
A standard hotel with modern amenities. The rooms look nice online. Note: the atmosphere is more functional than charming, and service may be less personal than at smaller properties.
